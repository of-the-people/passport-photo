Passport Photo Rules (US & ICAO)

  • Size: Exact 2×2 inches (51×51 mm) square.
  • Head Size: Head from chin to top of hair must be 1" to 1 3/8" (50%–69% of height).
  • Background: Plain white or off-white with no shadows.
  • Appearance: Neutral expression, both eyes open, no glasses or hats.
